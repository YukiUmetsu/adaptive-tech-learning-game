# References

## Learning, forgetting, and knowledge tracing

1. Settles, B., & Meeder, B. (2016). **A Trainable Spaced Repetition Model for Language Learning.** ACL 2016.  
   HLR; learned memory half-life; operational recall prediction.  
   <https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174/>  
   DOI: <https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/P16-1174>

2. Choffin, B., Popineau, F., Bourda, Y., & Vie, J.-J. (2019). **DAS3H: Modeling Student Learning and Forgetting for Optimally Scheduling Distributed Practice of Skills.**  
   Multi-skill learning + forgetting with temporal practice features.  
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06873>

3. Piech, C., et al. (2015). **Deep Knowledge Tracing.** NeurIPS 2015.  
   RNN-based latent learner state.  
   <https://papers.nips.cc/paper/5654-deep-knowledge-tracing>

4. Ghosh, A., Heffernan, N., & Lan, A. S. (2020). **Context-Aware Attentive Knowledge Tracing.**  
   Attention-based KT with monotonic temporal decay and question context.  
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12324>

5. Chen, O., Paas, F., & Sweller, J. (2021). **Spacing and Interleaving Effects Require Distinct Theoretical Bases: a Systematic Review.** Educational Psychology Review.  
   <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-021-09613-w>

6. **Does Individual Performance Feedback Increase the Use of Retrieval Practice?** Educational Psychology Review (2021).  
   Discusses retrieval practice/testing effect and feedback.  
   <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x>

## Gamification

7. Sailer, M., & Homner, L. (2020). **The Gamification of Learning: a Meta-analysis.** Educational Psychology Review 32, 77-112.  
   Reported small positive effects on cognitive, motivational, and behavioral outcomes.  
   <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w>

8. Li, L., Hew, K. F., & Du, J. (2024). **Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review.**  
   <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7>

9. Li, M., Ma, S., & Shi, Y. (2023). **Examining the effectiveness of gamification as a tool promoting teaching and learning in educational settings: a meta-analysis.** Frontiers in Psychology 14.  
   <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1253549>

10. **Does gamification improve student learning outcome? Evidence from a meta-analysis and synthesis of qualitative data in educational contexts.**  
    Reports benefits and also potential anxiety/jealousy; highlights feedback and goal setting.  
    <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1747938X19302908>

## Additional ML evaluation and scheduling references

26. Liu, Q., et al. (2022). **pyKT: A Python Library to Benchmark Deep Learning based Knowledge Tracing Models.** NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks.  
    Standardized KT evaluation; highlights leakage/evaluation-protocol issues.  
    <https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/75ca2b23d9794f02a92449af65a57556-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html>

27. Open Spaced Repetition. **SRS Benchmark.**  
    Engineering benchmark for FSRS and other spaced-repetition algorithms; useful as a baseline, not a substitute for app-specific evaluation.  
    <https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/srs-benchmark>

28. **LefoKT: Knowledge Tracing with Learnable Forgetting-aware Attention.** AAAI 2025.  
    Concrete later candidate for forgetting-aware deep KT.  
    <https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/34998>

29. **KARL: Knowledge-Aware Retrieval for Learning / content-aware scheduling research direction.**  
    Relevant to separating student modeling and teaching policy with semantically rich content.  
    <https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12291>
